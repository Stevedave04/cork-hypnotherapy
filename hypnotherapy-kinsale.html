Hypnotherapy in Kinsale

Clinical hypnotherapy about forty-five minutes from Kinsale, at the clinic in Ardfield near Clonakilty, or online from wherever suits you better.

Getting Here

Along the coast road from Kinsale

Kinsale is about thirty-one kilometres from the clinic at Ardfield, following the coast west through Ballinspittle and Timoleague. It is the most easterly of the towns served, and the closest to Cork city and the airport.

The R600 coast road takes roughly forty-five minutes and is a pleasant drive rather than a chore. Being further east, Kinsale clients sometimes combine an appointment with other business in Clonakilty.

What This Means For You

Performance, pressure and public-facing work

Kinsale has a high concentration of hospitality and customer-facing business, and the pressures that come with that are well represented in the work: presenting and speaking with composure, sleep that suffers during a demanding season, and confidence that has quietly eroded. The corporate and workplace side of the practice is directly relevant here.
