Hypnotherapy in Dunmanway

Clinical hypnotherapy about thirty minutes from Dunmanway, at the clinic in Ardfield near Clonakilty, or online from wherever suits you better.

Getting Here

Down from inland West Cork to the coast

Dunmanway is around twenty-two kilometres from the clinic at Ardfield, coming down from inland West Cork towards the coast. It is one of the shorter trips of the towns served, despite feeling further because the roads are quieter.

The route runs south-east towards Clonakilty and then out the Ardfield road, about thirty minutes. The roads are largely rural, so the drive is generally predictable outside of poor weather.

What This Means For You

When getting to appointments is the hard part

For people living in the more rural parts of West Cork, the practical barrier is often simply getting to a therapist at all, rather than any doubt about the therapy. That is worth naming, because it shapes what a sensible plan looks like: fewer, longer in-clinic sessions where that suits, and online for the rest.

All services on the treatments page are available either way. Frequently worked on:

  • Sleep problems and insomnia
  • Anxiety and stress
  • Stopping smoking
  • Weight loss using the SIMOS technique
  • Confidence and self-esteem
  • Stroke recovery support

Work with sports clubs and athletes and with businesses and teams is also available across West Cork.

Online Sessions

Built for exactly this situation

Online sessions were not an afterthought here. For inland West Cork they are frequently the difference between doing the work and not doing it, and they are held to the same standard as anything done in the room.
