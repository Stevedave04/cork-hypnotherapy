Hypnotherapy in Skibbereen

Clinical hypnotherapy roughly 35 minutes from Skibbereen, at the clinic in Ardfield near Clonakilty, or online from wherever you are.

Getting Here

Where the clinic is, and what the trip looks like

Skibbereen is around 25 kilometres from the clinic at Ardfield, near Clonakilty, along the N71. It is a comfortable run for an appointment, and for people who would rather not make it regularly, online sessions cover exactly the same ground.

The N71 connects Skibbereen and Clonakilty directly, and the clinic is a short turn off that route at Ardfield. Allow roughly thirty-five minutes. Because it is a longer trip than a local appointment, sessions can usually be arranged to suit the drive rather than squeezed into a lunch hour.

What People Come For

What we work on

Skibbereen clients frequently opt for a mixed approach: the first session in clinic, so that everything is established face to face, and later sessions online. The research on gut-directed and anxiety work shows no meaningful difference in outcome between the two.

The full range is on the treatments page, but the most common reasons people get in touch are:

  • Anxiety and stress
  • Stopping smoking
  • Weight loss using the SIMOS technique
  • Sleep problems and insomnia
  • Confidence and self-esteem
  • IBS and chronic pain

Work with sports clubs and athletes and with businesses and teams is also available across West Cork.
