Hypnotherapy in Skibbereen

Clinical hypnotherapy around thirty-five minutes from Skibbereen, at the clinic in Ardfield near Clonakilty, or online from wherever suits you better.

Getting Here

A straightforward run up the N71

Skibbereen is about twenty-five kilometres from the clinic at Ardfield, near Clonakilty. The N71 links the two directly, so it is a familiar road rather than a complicated cross-country trip.

Allow around thirty-five minutes, depending on how you find Rosscarbery and Leap on the day. Appointments can be timed to avoid the slowest part of the afternoon rather than squeezed into a lunch break.

What This Means For You

A mix of in clinic and online tends to work best

For a thirty-five minute drive, many people from the Skibbereen direction do the first session in clinic, so everything is established face to face, and then move later sessions online. The evidence on gut-directed and anxiety work shows no meaningful drop in outcome when sessions run over video.

  • IBS and chronic pain
  • Anxiety and stress
  • Stopping smoking
  • Sleep problems and insomnia

  • Phobias and fears

Work with sports clubs and athletes and with businesses and teams is also available across West Cork.
