Why Online Works

The Benefits of Online Hypnotherapy

Many clients now prefer online sessions for reasons that go well beyond simple convenience.

Your Own Environment

Being in a familiar, comfortable space can actually deepen relaxation and make the hypnotic state easier to achieve. Many clients find home sessions more calming than travelling to a clinic.

Flexible Scheduling

Without travel time, fitting a session around work, childcare, or a busy schedule becomes far more manageable. Evening and weekend slots are readily available for online clients.

Nationwide Access

Quality hypnotherapy is not equally accessible everywhere in Ireland. Online sessions mean clients in rural areas, other counties, or abroad can access the same professional standard of care.

Same Clinical Results

Research and clinical experience consistently show that online hypnotherapy produces outcomes equivalent to in-person sessions. The therapeutic relationship and the hypnotic process translate fully to the online environment.

Fully Confidential

Sessions are conducted on encrypted, GDPR-compliant video platforms. Your privacy is protected to the same standard as in-clinic consultations.

No Special Equipment

A laptop, tablet, or smartphone with a camera and microphone is all you need. We will walk you through the technical setup at your free initial consultation so you feel confident before your first session.

Getting Started

How to Prepare for Your Online Session

A little preparation makes a real difference to the quality of your online hypnotherapy experience.

1

Find a Private Space

Choose a room where you will not be interrupted for the duration of the session. Let others in your household know not to disturb you. Put your phone on silent.

2

Get Comfortable

Sit in a comfortable chair or lie on a sofa or bed. Have a blanket to hand if you tend to feel cool when relaxing deeply. Loose, comfortable clothing works best.

3

Check Your Connection

A stable Wi-Fi connection is preferable to mobile data. Test your camera and microphone beforehand. We will send you a link to your session room the day before your appointment.

4

Use Headphones

Good quality headphones or earphones significantly improve the session experience. They help you focus on the therapist's voice, reduce ambient noise, and deepen relaxation.
