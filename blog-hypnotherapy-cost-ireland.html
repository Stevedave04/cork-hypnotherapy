How Much Does Hypnotherapy Cost in Ireland?

John A O'Connor, Clinical Hypnotherapist 24 June 2026 7 minute read

Almost nobody asks this question out loud on a first call, and almost everybody wants to know. It is a fair question, and the reason it is hard to answer in a single number is that hypnotherapy is not one product.

This article explains what actually drives the cost, roughly how many sessions different issues tend to need, and the questions worth asking any therapist before you book. For current fees at this practice, the honest answer is to ask, because the right programme depends on what you are bringing.

Why there is no single price

A stop smoking programme and a course of work for long-standing anxiety are different pieces of work. One is often completed in a single extended session. The other typically runs to several appointments spaced over weeks. Quoting one figure for both would be misleading.

Cost in practice is a function of three things: how many sessions the issue realistically needs, how long each session runs, and the training and experience of the person delivering it.

How many sessions different issues tend to need

This is the single biggest factor, and it is the one you can estimate before booking.

At this practice most clients complete a course of between three and eight sessions. Smoking cessation is often achieved in a single extended session of around two hours. Work that involves long-standing patterns, such as anxiety, confidence or sleep, generally sits in the middle of that range, because the spacing between sessions is part of how it works.

Gut-directed work for IBS is the outlier: the protocol developed through the Manchester research typically runs to six to twelve sessions, and that is what the evidence base is built on. Fewer sessions is not a cheaper version of it, it is a different thing.

What session length actually means

Hypnotherapy sessions are usually longer than a standard counselling hour. A typical session runs 50 to 60 minutes, and initial or intensive sessions run longer. A stop smoking session of around two hours is doing in one appointment what would otherwise be spread across several.

When comparing prices between practitioners, compare the length and the number of sessions together rather than the headline figure. A lower per-session price across ten sessions is not cheaper than a higher price across four.

Training, accreditation and why it shows up in the price

Hypnotherapy is not a statutorily regulated profession in Ireland, which means the range of training behind the title is very wide. Some practitioners have completed a weekend course. Others hold accredited diplomas, work under a professional body's code of ethics, carry professional indemnity insurance and undertake continuing professional development.

That difference is real and it is reflected in fees. It is also the difference that matters most if something complex comes up in a session.

What is usually included

Ask what the fee covers, because practices differ. Things worth checking: whether the initial consultation is charged separately, whether recordings for use between sessions are included, whether follow-up contact is included, and whether there is a package rate for a course rather than a per-session rate.

At this practice the initial 30 minute discovery call is free and carries no obligation, which exists precisely so that you can establish what a realistic programme looks like before any money changes hands.

Is it covered by insurance or the HSE?

HSE access to hypnotherapy is limited and it does not sit inside standard care pathways. Most people in Ireland access it privately.

Some private health insurers will contribute towards complementary therapies as part of a wider benefit, often requiring the practitioner to be a member of a recognised professional body. It is worth checking your policy directly, and asking your insurer what documentation they need.

Questions worth asking before you book

A short list that will tell you more than a price comparison will:

How many sessions do you estimate for what I have described? What happens if it takes fewer, or more? What are your qualifications and which body accredits you? Are you insured? What is your cancellation policy? Do you offer anything between sessions?

A practitioner who answers those directly, including being willing to say that they are not the right fit, is generally a good sign.

The cheapest programme is the one that works. Six sessions that resolve something you have carried for fifteen years is better value than two that do not, regardless of the per-session figure.

Frequently asked questions

Is hypnotherapy expensive compared to other therapies?
Per session it is often comparable to private counselling or CBT. The difference is usually the number of sessions: hypnotherapy courses tend to be shorter, so the total can be lower even where the per-session rate is higher.
Do I pay for the initial consultation?
At this practice the 30 minute discovery call is free and carries no obligation. It exists so you can find out what a realistic programme looks like before committing to anything.
Will I be told how many sessions I need up front?
You will be given a realistic estimate based on what you describe, with the caveat that people differ. Anyone who guarantees an exact number before meeting you is overpromising.
Is online cheaper than in clinic?
Not usually, because the session itself is the same length and the same work. What online saves is travel time and cost, which for someone in Bantry or Skibbereen can be significant.
