Your Therapist

John A O'Connor: Over 12 Years Helping Cork & Beyond

Welcome. If you are reading this, something has probably brought you to a point where you are ready to try something different. I have spent more than twelve years working with people who feel exactly that way: anxious, stuck, or exhausted by a pattern they cannot break on their own. My name is John A O'Connor. I am a clinical hypnotherapist based in Clonakilty, Co. Cork, and helping people move past what is holding them back is the work I find most meaningful.

I am an accredited member of the European Association of Professional Hypnotherapists (EAPH) and hold additional qualifications in cognitive behavioural hypnotherapy (CBH) and solution-focused brief therapy (SFBT). I undertake regular continuing professional development to ensure my practice reflects the latest evidence-based methods.

I work with adults and teenagers on issues ranging from anxiety and phobias to smoking cessation, weight loss (SIMOS), and confidence. Every client I see receives my full attention and a programme built around their unique circumstances.

My Approach

Collaborative, Evidence-Informed, and Uniquely Yours

No two clients are the same, so no two treatment programmes are the same. Before we begin any hypnotherapy work, I invest time in understanding your history, your goals, and what has and has not worked for you in the past.

My sessions draw on clinical hypnotherapy, cognitive behavioural techniques, and solution-focused principles. This combination means we are not only working with your subconscious during trance but also equipping you with practical conscious tools to support your progress between sessions.

I believe strongly in informed consent. I will always explain exactly what a technique involves and why I am recommending it. You are in control at every step of the process.
