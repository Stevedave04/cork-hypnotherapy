Why Choose Hypnotherapy

You Have Tried All the Other Things. This Works Differently.

A calmer, more confident version of you is possible, and it starts with understanding why the old approaches did not stick.

Most people who come to see John A have already tried many different therapies. They have read the books, done the breathing exercises, and told themselves it would get better on its own. It has not. That is not a failure of willpower. It is the difference between your conscious mind and the part of the brain where patterns are actually stored.

At Cork Hypnotherapy, trading as O Connor Clinical Therapies, clinical hypnotherapy is the core of the work, supported where helpful by complementary approaches including NLP (Neuro Linguistic Programming), SBT (Superconscious Breakthrough Therapy), Reiki, Pranic Healing, and somatic therapy. We work directly with your mind: the parts other approaches often cannot reach. Unlike therapies that only teach you to manage symptoms, we help you unlearn the patterns causing them in the first place.
