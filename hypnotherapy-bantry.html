Hypnotherapy in Bantry

Clinical hypnotherapy around fifty-five minutes from Bantry, at the clinic in Ardfield near Clonakilty, or online from wherever suits you better.

Getting Here

The longest trip of the West Cork towns

Bantry is around thirty-nine kilometres from the clinic at Ardfield, which makes it the furthest of the towns served and, realistically, the one where a weekly drive is hard to justify.

Depending on whether you come via the N71 through Skibbereen or across through Dunmanway, allow around fifty-five minutes each way. That is close to two hours of driving around a one hour appointment.

What This Means For You

An honest recommendation for this distance

Rather than pretend the distance does not matter, the sensible approach from Bantry is usually online for anything that runs to a course of sessions, and in clinic only where a single long appointment does the job. Stop smoking is the clearest example: one extended session rather than six, which makes the trip worth making once.

All of the following are available online, and on the treatments page in full:

  • Stopping smoking
  • Anxiety and stress
  • Sleep problems and insomnia
  • Weight loss using the SIMOS technique
  • IBS and chronic pain
  • Confidence and self-esteem

Work with sports clubs and athletes and with businesses and teams is also available across West Cork.

Online Sessions

Online is the default here, not the fallback

For the Bantry and Beara direction, online sessions are the primary way the practice works, and they are treated as the main service rather than a lesser version of it. Outcomes in the research are comparable, and nothing about the session structure is shortened or simplified.

There is more on the online sessions page, and an honest look at what the research actually shows in this article.

Common Questions

Questions we get from Bantry

Is it realistic to travel from Bantry for a course of sessions?
Honestly, for most people it is not, and online is the better answer. The exception is work that is completed in a single extended session, such as stopping smoking.
Does online really work as well?
For the conditions this practice treats, the research finds comparable outcomes. Hypnotherapy works through focused attention and language, neither of which requires being in the same room.
What if I want to meet in person at least once?
That can be arranged for the first appointment, with the remainder of the course online.
